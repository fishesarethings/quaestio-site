# Quaestio — Monetization Ideas (keep the core free, earn on the edges)

Your pitch is "free, no paywall" — keep the core (levels, moderation, AI) free forever.
That honesty is your brand advantage. Monetize the extras, not the essentials:

## 1. Donations (zero work, honest)
- **Ko-fi** / **Buy Me a Coffee** — a "support the server you love" button in `/about` and the invite flow.
- **Patreon** free tier — throwback perks (a thank-you role) for supporters.

## 2. Premium cosmetics (keep everything functional free)
- Tier on Ko-fi/Patreon that unlocks *cosmetic only*: custom level-up card images, accent color themes, custom rank titles, extra tag slots.
- NOT paywalling: moderation, anti-spam, AI basics. Never gate safety features — Discord and your users both care.

## 3. AI priority lane
- Free: standard queue (one at a time, as now).
- Premium: priority queue / longer replies / bigger model. This monetizes exactly the resource scarcity you already have — legitimately.

## 4. One-time commissions
- **Custom bot builds** for businesses/communities — this is where real money is ($200–$2,000/job). Quaestio is your portfolio piece. Add `/quote`? No — just link a "commission me" page in `/about`.

## 5. Hosted instance (SaaS-lite, monthly)
- Run Quaestio for server owners who can't self-host. ~$3–5/mo per server.
- Caveats: becomes a real business (support, uptime, DPA). Start with 1–3 servers, not a platform.

## 6. White-label deployments
- Streamers/communities pay a one-time fee to have Quaestio rebranded (name, logo, colors) on their own box. No recurring support burden for you.

## 7. Affiliate/partner referrals
- `/refer` command: recommend hosting (your box is on...), VPNs, game deals. Passive small income. Keep it opt-in and transparent.

## 8. Marketplace goodies (once you have users)
- Ko-fi shop: level-up card templates, emoji packs, theme presets, custom welcome-card art. Low effort, repeatable.

## 9. top.gg premium
- Once you have reach, top.gg lets you list with premium features (earlier, more visible) — small but real.

## Order of attack (recommended)
1. **Ko-fi donations + link** in `/about` (today, 10 min).
2. **Premium cosmetics tier** once you have ~50 servers.
3. **Commissions** — list a portfolio, take 1–2 paid bot gigs.
4. Revisit hosted instance only if commissions overflow.

## Compliance reminders (so you keep the badge + money)
- Discord ToS allows monetizing apps, but: no paywalling basic functionality, no deceptive upsells, disclose premium features honestly.
- Money received + verified bot = Discord may ask for tax/payment info — keep records.
- Don't sell data, ever (it's also your marketing line).
